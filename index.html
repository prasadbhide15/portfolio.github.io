WK · ANALYTICS

Finding the hidden blind spot with Pendo

Wolters Kluwer — Product Manager 60% +
Problem

Support tickets weren't surfacing a friction point in the product — a gap between what customers reported and what they were actually struggling with.

Approach

Used Pendo product analytics to study real user behavior across the platform rather than relying on reported issues alone, tracing usage patterns back to a specific workflow.

Result

Uncovered that 60% of users were struggling to upload and manage attachments, and turned the finding into a prioritized fix on the roadmap.

OB · PROCESS

Reducing design churn by 90% with low-fidelity testing

Omni Bridge — Product Owner −90% +
Problem

UI designs were changing significantly mid-development, burning engineering time on rework that could have been caught earlier.

Approach

Introduced a practice of demoing low-fidelity mockups to end users before development began, catching misalignment while it was still cheap to fix.

Result

Cut UI design changes during development by 90%, protecting engineering time and shortening the path from idea to shipped feature.
